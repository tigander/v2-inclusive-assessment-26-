Transparency & Data Care

System & Data Privacy: Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash. Processing is strictly ephemeral. The text you enter is processed securely in real-time and is never stored, saved, or used to train AI models.

Sources Consulted: Feedback is informed by New Zealand UDL and inclusive assessment guidance, including Ministry of Education resources, Teaching Council guidance, NZCER research, and selected Ako Aotearoa and peer-reviewed resources on neurodiversity and culturally responsive practice.

Advisory Use Only: This tool analyses initial assessment drafts to provide formative feedback. It does not make high-stakes decisions. Human oversight and educator judgement are strictly required.

Escalation: If evidence for an accommodation is mixed or you are unsure, please consult your school's inclusive education lead or SENCO.
